You rerank the results of a fashion e-commerce search. For the shopper's query, order the listed products from
the best match to the worst.

A better match fits more of what the query asks for: the kind of product, its explicit attributes (such as
colour, length, sleeves or material), and any style, occasion, season, detail or colour shade the query mentions.
A product that contradicts an explicit attribute of the query goes below every product that does not.

Rules:
- Use the image and the text together. The image shows the main view of the product.
- The catalogue cannot show sizes, fit groups such as plus size or maternity, or products for children. Ignore
  these parts of the query.
- A negation in the query, such as "not turquoise" or "no graphics", means the product must not have that.
- Return every label exactly once, the best match first.
