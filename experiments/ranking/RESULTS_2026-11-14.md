# Ranking task: results

Answer to the ranking task of 2026-11-13 (`TASK_RANKING.md`, kept outside the repository): find a ranking for
`search_ui` that is better than `hybrid`, or simpler and as good. Measured on 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-14 on the 313
dev queries of the benchmark, on the branch `ranking-fable`. The test split was not used.

## Summary

- **Recommendation: the new ranking `sentences`, now the default of `search_ui`.** It compares the whole query and
  every residual phrase with the single sentences of a product's description, keeps the concept scores of `hybrid`,
  drops its colour and detail matching, and uses fitted weights instead of hand-set ones.
- **Quality.** nDCG@10 0.855 against 0.837 for `hybrid`: **+0.018, 95% interval +0.005 to +0.031**, with weights that
  never saw the query being ranked. Against the held-out `hybrid`, with the held-out bank: +0.017 [+0.005, +0.029].
  MRR and P@1 are the same as for `hybrid` (0.903 and 0.871 against 0.901 and 0.871).
- **A second judge from another model family agrees**: +0.022 [+0.011, +0.034], and the order of the rankings holds.
- **Simpler.** The default ranking no longer uses 19 hand-set thresholds and weights of `hybrid` (list below). It
  uses 6 fitted weights and no new threshold.
- **Latency.** It adds 33 ms (p50) and 149 ms (p95) after the parser returns, against 29 ms and 413 ms for
  `hybrid`. 4% of the queries need an embedding call after the parser, against 9%.
- **One group gets worse.** On queries with an occasion phrase ("for work"), `sentences` is below `hybrid`:
  0.859 against 0.879, −0.020 [−0.040, −0.002].
- **Room left.** An LLM that reorders the top 20 of `hybrid` reaches 0.900. `sentences` closes 29% of that gap.
- **Spent: $4.80 of $20** (`SPEND.md`).

## What was measured and how

- **One code version.** All rankings of `evaluate.py` were rerun with the current code first. The `bank` rankings
  could not run with it: `evaluate.order()` did not pass the filters that `server.rank("bank")` reads. That is fixed.
- **Grades.** The judge is unchanged (Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, thinking MEDIUM, prompt version 809237bbedeb). Every
  product in every top 10 of every ranking below is graded: the report shows 0 ungraded products for all 82
  rankings. The dev queries now have 12,777 graded pairs, 4,874 more than at the start: grade 0 1,582 (12%),
  1 5,607 (44%), 2 1,185 (9%), 3 4,403 (34%).
- **The numbers are lower than in the task.** `hybrid` had 0.885 there and has 0.837 here. Nothing got worse:
  nDCG compares a top 10 with the best order of all graded products, and the new grades found more products of
  grade 3, mostly in positions 11–20 of `hybrid`. All rankings below are compared on the same grades.
- **Query counts.** nDCG is over 312 queries (one has no product above grade 0), MRR and P@1 over 263.
- **Fitted weights are reported out of fold.** The queries are split into 5 folds by a hash of their id. The
  weights that rank a query were fitted without the queries of its fold (`learn.py`, `evaluate.fold`).
  `sentences_fitted` uses the weights the search UI ships, fitted on all dev queries; on dev it is in sample and
  is shown only for reference.
- **Harness changes.** `evaluate.py` caches query-time embeddings on disk (`cache.py`), so a rerun is free and gives
  the same vectors; the search UI still caches nothing. It also runs BLAS with one thread: with cached embeddings,
  eight worker threads calling threaded BLAS stalled the run. The report now lists every ranking found in
  `data/runs.jsonl`, with differences to `combined`, `hybrid_details_w0.25` and `hybrid_details_heldout_w0.25`.
- The full report of all 82 rankings is `report_dev_2026-11-14.md`. Its time table is not latency: the embeddings
  came from the cache. Latency is measured separately below. `hybrid_top20` in it is the input of the ceiling, the
  top 20 of `hybrid`; its top 10 is that of `hybrid`.

## Ceiling

`ceiling.py`. For every dev query, Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite (thinking MEDIUM) got the top 20 of `hybrid` with texts
and photos and returned them in order, best first. It ran offline only. Cost $1.98, about $0.006 per query.

- **`hybrid_llm_rerank`: 0.900**, +0.063 over `hybrid`.
- **`hybrid_oracle`: 0.948.** The same 20 products ordered by the judge's own grades: the most any reranker of
  the top 20 could reach.
- The reranker is the judge's model, so part of its gain is the model agreeing with itself. With the second judge
  its gain over `hybrid` is +0.055 instead of +0.067 on the same products (see Circularity).
- The gap was not in the weights. Other weights for the terms of `hybrid`, and fitted weights on the signals it
  already had, stayed within ±0.006 of it (variants below). The gap was largest for shades (0.845 against 0.959 for
  the reranker) and details (0.812 against 0.897): things the description states in one sentence and its embedding
  blurs.

## The sentence ranking

Code: `search_ui/core/sentences.py`, `bank.sentence_terms`, `server.SearchEngine.sentence_columns`. Description:
`search_ui/README_ranking.md`.

```
sentences = 1.00 × z(description) + 1.40 × z(best sentences) + 2.52 × z(image) + 0.26 × z(features)
          + 2.94 × z(concept scores of each occasion, style or season phrase)
          + 43.0 × (best sentence of each phrase, above the phrase's median)
```

- **Product sentences.** The name and every sentence of the description of every product are embedded one by one:
  30,977 unique texts, $0.06, 68 MB with 768 dimensions.
- **Whole query.** The mean similarity of the query to the two best sentences of a candidate, as a z-score.
- **Phrase.** The similarity of a residual phrase to the candidate's best sentence, minus the phrase's median
  similarity to all sentences, 0 when below. The median replaces a threshold: a phrase that no sentence speaks about
  gives nearly the same small value to every candidate.
- **Phrase bank.** Used for three things only: the concepts of an occasion, style or season phrase, negation, and
  skipping size and audience phrases. Its colour and detail routes are not used.
- **Weights.** Pairwise logistic regression on the judge's grades. Over the 5 folds they range: best sentences
  1.14–2.04, image 2.22–3.23, features 0.07–0.41, concept 2.40–4.81, phrase 25.0–59.6, each relative to the
  description. The out-of-fold score (0.855) is close to the in-sample one (0.857), so 6 weights on 313 queries do
  not overfit much.

  without the sentence term, so the cause is the fitted weights, not the sentence signal. It is still +0.042 above

- **By number of candidates.** The gain is in queries with 10 or more candidates: +0.029 [−0.000, +0.060] for
  10–49, +0.019 [+0.002, +0.036] for 50 and more, 0.000 for 2–9, where even a random order has 0.915.

What the table says:
- **The signals of `hybrid` were used up.** Other weights, fitted weights and soft thresholds all stay within
  ±0.006 of it. Only removing the concept term hurts (−0.011).
- **The two sentence parts carry the gain.** Without both, the lean ranking is at `hybrid` (0.834). The whole-query
  part adds more (0.847 → 0.855) than the phrase part (0.851 → 0.855), and together they give +0.021 over the lean
  ranking without them.
- **The colour and detail machinery adds nothing once the sentences are in.** `v_fitted_hybrid_sentences` keeps every
  term of `hybrid` and reaches 0.856, the same as `sentences` (0.855) with far fewer rules.
- **Fitting matters as much as the signal.** With the hand weights of `hybrid`, the lean ranking only equals `hybrid`
  (0.837); `hybrid_sentence` gets +0.006.
- **Nothing added on top helps.** Soft colours, filter fit, the name, the image, TF-IDF or another way of fitting
  all land between 0.852 and 0.857, inside the noise. None was kept.
- **A sentence term as a plain z-score fails** (`v_hybrid_sentence_z0.5`, −0.018): for a phrase without a matching
  sentence the z-score turns noise into a full-size term. The median floor fixes that.

Measured once and not kept in `variants.py`: sentence embeddings with 3,072, 1,536 and 768 dimensions gave nDCG
0.854, 0.853 and 0.854 for an earlier form of the ranking with seven parts, on the grades of that moment. 768 is
used: it is four times smaller and faster.

## Circularity

- **Risk.** The judge is Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, which also made the concept scores, colours and details. The new
  signals do not come from it: the sentence embeddings are from the embedding model, like the description
  embeddings. But the six weights are fitted on this judge's grades, and 4,874 grades were added, so `sentences`
  could have learnt the judge's taste.
- **Second judge.** `judge2.py`: `anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5` through OpenRouter, with the same prompt and layout,
  graded every product in the top 10 of ten rankings for all 313 dev queries: 7,608 pairs, $0.35.
- **Agreement.** Same grade 67.9%; within one grade 96.2%; same side of the relevance line 87.4%; quadratic weighted
  kappa 0.785. The mean grade is the same (1.76 and 1.77). The second judge gives 2 more often where the first
  gives 3 (662 pairs) and 1 where the first gives 0 (560 pairs).

## Latency and API calls

`latency.py`, all 313 dev queries through `SearchEngine.search`.

With real embedding calls. The parser is replaced by a wait as long as the real parser took on a query of the
parser benchmark (p50 0.72 s, p95 1.04 s), then the benchmark parse is returned. "After the parser" is the total
time minus that wait: filtering, waiting for embeddings that are not ready, the ranking, and building the list:

| ranking | after the parser, ms: p50 / p95 / max | of which the ranking step: p50 / p95 / max | embedding calls per query: p50 / p95 / max | queries with a call after the parser |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| combined | 26 / 47 / 605 | 2 / 11 / 550 | 2 / 2 / 2 | 0 |
| hybrid | 29 / 413 / 2,197 | 4 / 386 / 2,179 | 12 / 22 / 33 | 29 (9%) |
| **sentences** | **33 / 149 / 671** | 8 / 120 / 647 | 11 / 22 / 32 | 14 (4%) |

Computation only, with embeddings served from memory:

| ranking | after the parser, ms: p50 / p95 / max | of which the ranking step: p50 / p95 / max |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| combined | 18 / 44 / 185 | 3 / 12 / 42 |
| hybrid | 24 / 53 / 201 | 7 / 21 / 44 |
| sentences | 29 / 59 / 257 | 12 / 28 / 60 |

- **Within the limit of about 200 ms at p95** (149 ms). `hybrid` is not (413 ms), because 9% of its queries wait
  for an embedding after the parser.
- **Fewer late calls.** A phrase of the bank now uses the embedding stored with the bank, and a known detail phrase
  no longer needs its English version embedded. A late call remains for a phrase that the bank does not know and
  that is not a span of one to three words of the query.
- **The number of embedding calls did not go down** (p50 11). Most of them are the spans of the query embedded
  while the parser runs, before anyone knows which of them is a residual phrase.
- **Memory.** 68 MB on disk and 95 MB in memory for the sentence embeddings.

## Limits of these results

- **One dev set, many variants.** About 40 variants were compared on the same 313 queries, and the recommended one
  was picked among them. Its out-of-fold score is fair for the weights, not for the choice of the variant. The test
  split is untouched and should confirm the result at the milestone.
- **The gain is moderate.** +0.018 of the +0.063 that the LLM reranker shows. Most of what is left is not reachable
  from the signals tried here: themed queries with no matching product (their grades are noisy), an excluded colour
  that appears as an accent ("no red" and a red logo), and judgement calls on style.
- **Negation is still unmeasured**: about 3 negated phrases and 4 negative colour filters in dev.

## Not tried

- **Typed residual phrases from the parser.** It needs a change of the parser prompt and paid parser calls. Its
  possible gain is smaller now: the sentence ranking needs from the bank only the concepts, the negation and the
  skipped phrases, and it scores the same with the held-out bank.
- **A small local embedding model** for the phrases. The one attempt to load a model on this machine was killed for
  lack of memory. It would remove the span embeddings (p50 11 calls) and the late calls, but the sentence
  embeddings would have to come from the same local model.
- **The 1,024 px photos.** Not merged into `pilot9922`; concept scores and photo embeddings are from the small ones.
